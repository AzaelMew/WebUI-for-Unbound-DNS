Supported formats, safety limits & DNS filtering limits

Supports UTF-8 plain domains, blocking hosts entries (0.0.0.0, 127.0.0.1, ::, ::1), simple ||domain^ Adblock rules, and RPZ QNAME NXDOMAIN (CNAME .) records with supported SOA/NS, origin and TTL metadata. Auto-detect examines each source; choose an explicit format when the feed is known.
